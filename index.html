About

Turning complex data into useful decisions.

I am an applied data scientist with an M.S. in Applied Data Science and a biomedical engineering focus from the University of Florida. I build reliable, reproducible workflows across Python, SQL, machine learning, statistical analysis, and bioinformatics.

My recent work includes genetic variant classification, regulatory DNA modeling, cardiovascular risk prediction, and operational reporting. I care about the full path from messy data to a clear result: validating the inputs, choosing an interpretable approach, documenting the work, and communicating what the analysis can and cannot support.

Before graduate school, I served eight years in the U.S. Army. That experience shaped my approach to technical work with discipline, accountability, adaptability, and a systems view of how teams get important work done.
